(02) — About

Engineering is an act of trust.

I’m William Edwardo Gunawan, a Forward Deployed Engineer in Toronto. I graduated from Toronto Metropolitan University with a Bachelor of Computer Science with Distinction, shipped at startups like Fiscal AI and Postilize, and build and run production software for clients. I work directly with the teams who use what I build.

Off-screen — music, games, travel, food

Philosophy

  1. 01

    Start with the user, not the stack.

    I sit close to the people who will use the software. Their workflow decides the architecture, and the tools come after.

  2. 02

    Ship, then sharpen.

    Small releases, real feedback, steady iteration. A working version in front of people beats a perfect plan nobody has seen.

  3. 03

    Own it after launch.

    Monitoring, on-call, fixing what breaks. The job doesn’t end at deploy. That’s where people start depending on it.

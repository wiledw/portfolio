How to explore

  • WASD or arrows walk
  • Shift run
  • E / Space talk, read, open
  • 1–9 open a link in a dialog
  • B hop on or off your bike (it's parked by the post box)
  • Click / tap walk to a spot
  • M map tap a place to go there
  • Scroll zoom
  • Esc close

Where things are

  • Everything is on one street. Walk along it and each place offers a prompt.
  • West: home (about me, with Mochi napping across the street) and the rice paddies (skills).
  • East: the workshop (Central Auto Sales) and the station, one stop board per role, oldest first.
  • North, up the steps: the shrine, with a plaque for each hackathon win.
  • South, over the bridge: the post office (contact) and the bus back to the classic site.
  • Tap any place on the map to walk there.
